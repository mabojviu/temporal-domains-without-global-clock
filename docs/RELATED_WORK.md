# Relation to the previous JMP manuscript

Related manuscript:

**Serialization Gauge and Conditioned-Hazard Obstructions for Finite Quantum-Jump Event Kernels**
Journal of Mathematical Physics submission identifier: `JMP26-AR-02157`.

The two works address different questions.

The earlier manuscript studies serialization-gauge and conditioned-hazard obstructions in finite
quantum-jump event kernels.

The present manuscript studies temporal domains, typed partial gluing, affine holonomy,
relational Hodge structure, and provenance-aware temporal transport/divergence.

The present repository is therefore a distinct reproducibility record rather than a replacement
or revision of the earlier work.
