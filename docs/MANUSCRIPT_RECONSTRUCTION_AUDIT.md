# Manuscript reconstruction audit — broadened Article 2

## Purpose

This note records why the second LQTA-D manuscript is being reconstructed rather than merely extended. The earlier public manuscript was scientifically coherent but narratively too narrow: it centered temporal domains, CERT-016/CERT-017, and TC9 field structure, while much of the successor chain that made those results meaningful appeared only as lineage metadata or background.

No frozen certificate, hash, gate, failed campaign, or predecessor claim is changed by this reconstruction.

## Main deficiency of the earlier manuscript

The earlier manuscript effectively began near the temporal-atlas / scalar-deconditioning / typed-domain part of the program. That ordering made temporal domains look like the primitive object and made the gauge-field construction look like the principal successor result.

The reconstructed manuscript instead follows the actual dependency chain:

`predecessor CERT-001..008 -> CERT-009 -> CERT-010 -> CERT-011 -> CERT-012 -> CERT-013 -> CERT-014 -> CERT-015 -> CERT-016 -> CERT-017 -> TC9A/B/C`

The arrows express dependency or conceptual inheritance, not equivalence of evidential status.

## Core thesis after reconstruction

The broadened manuscript is organized around the claim that, within the declared finite relational models, overlapping local dependencies can make temporal information collectively reconstructible and can generate examples of nontrivial collaborative temporal structure without a primitive scalar clock or global target oracle. Temporal domains are subsequently useful active-coordination and interaction patches; they are not the primitive definition of time.

Global scalarization is treated as an optional integrable reduction. Nonintegrable CAL structure can carry gauge-invariant holonomy/Hodge content, and provenance can select a finite current satisfying an exact continuity/balance law.

## Evidence classes kept separate

The manuscript explicitly separates:

- historical governance (CERT-009);
- executable finite qualification (CERT-010);
- exact finite theorem/construction (TC1–TC3 and other structural propositions);
- frozen external holdout qualification (CERT-011/012/014/015/016/017);
- semantic/global audit (CERT-013);
- exact algebraic foundations (TC8A, TC9A/B/C);
- interpretation and future physical hypotheses.

No single certificate is presented as a monotonic proof score.

## Nonclaims preserved

The reconstruction does not establish universal emergence of physical time, a continuum limit, relativistic spacetime, Lorentz symmetry, spatial dimension, gravity, Einstein dynamics, physical curvature, literal islands of time, causal influence from reconstructibility alone, or a Noether origin for the conditional balance of `Q_T`.
