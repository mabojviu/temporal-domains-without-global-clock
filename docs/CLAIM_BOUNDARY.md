# Claim boundary

## Supported in the present article

The archived finite relational framework supports:
- temporal coordination without a primitive global scalar clock;
- typed partial gluing across temporal domains;
- separation of connectivity from temporal semantics;
- affine CAL holonomy and scalarization obstruction;
- a gauge-covariant scale connection;
- exact/coexact/harmonic decomposition on registered relational complexes;
- gauge-invariant circulation on registered CAL 2-cells;

- a conditional conservation law for total nonintegrable temporal-calibration content in pure redistribution.

## Explicitly not supported

The repository does not establish:
- continuum spacetime;
- a preferred spatial geometry;
- Euclidean, hyperbolic, spherical, Lorentzian, or other primitive metric geometry;
- a derived dimension;
- Lorentz invariance;
- gravitation;
- an Einstein equation;
- a physical source/sink of time itself;
- a Noether derivation of Q_T.

## Geometry neutrality

No primitive coordinates, distances, angles, volumes, dimensions, or spatial embeddings are assigned.
Registered CAL 2-cells are compositional consistency cells, not spatial faces.

The unit-weight cochain inner product used in finite Hodge constructions is an auxiliary combinatorial
choice and is not interpreted as a physical spacetime metric.
