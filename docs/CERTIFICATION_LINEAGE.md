# Certification lineage and historical continuity

## Purpose

This repository is the public evidence archive for the second manuscript in a continuing LQTA-D temporal-structure program. The present article does not begin at CERT-015: it inherits a chain of governance, reduction, reconstructive-collaboration, domain-lifecycle, global-audit, noisy-atlas, scale-growth, relational-coherence, and typed-domain results.

The lineage below records that history without implying that every certificate proves the same claim.

## Relationship to the predecessor article

CERT-001 through CERT-008 belong to the predecessor research line associated with the manuscript **Serialization Gauge and Conditioned-Hazard Obstructions for Finite Quantum-Jump Event Kernels** (JMP submission `JMP26-AR-02157`). They are not duplicated as binary archives here because the present repository is a distinct successor record. Their role is historical and methodological: the successor program preserves the predecessor claim boundaries and does not retroactively modify its frozen certificates.

CERT-009 (`PASS_MASTER_SUCCESSOR_GOVERNANCE`) marks the governance boundary between the predecessor and successor programs. It is recorded here as a historical governance certificate; a standalone CERT-009 binary package is not duplicated in this archive.

## Logical reading

The chain should not be read as a single monotonic "proof score." Its role is dependency and historical provenance:

`predecessor CERT-001..008 -> CERT-009 governance -> CERT-010 interface/reduction -> CERT-011 collaboration -> CERT-012 lifecycle -> CERT-013 semantic audit -> CERT-014 noisy atlas -> CERT-015 scale growth -> CERT-016 relational deconditioning -> CERT-017 typed domain interaction -> TC9A/B/C field structure`.

Each arrow means **scientific dependence or conceptual inheritance**, not equivalence of claims.

## Negative-path history preserved

Three non-success states are archived because they materially shaped later frozen claims:

1. **TC4H v0.1 scientific FAIL** — retained as a genuine failed holdout/postmortem; CERT-012 comes from the prospectively repaired v0.2 campaign, not from retrospective rescue.
2. **TC5H v0.2 scientific FAIL** — retained as the failed noisy-atlas holdout; CERT-014 comes from the prospectively repaired v0.3.3 campaign with frozen gates preserved.
3. **TC8H v0.1 pre-science abandonment** — the matched-skeleton contract defect was found during preflight before science. It is not a scientific FAIL and its nonce/case-set were not reused; CERT-017 derives from the prospectively repaired v0.2 campaign.

These records are included to make the development path inspectable rather than presenting only successful endpoints.

## Claim-boundary rule

Historical continuity does not expand the claims of the present manuscript. In particular, none of CERT-009..017 or TC9A..C establishes continuum spacetime, a preferred spatial geometry, Lorentz symmetry, gravity, an Einstein equation, literal temporal islands in nature, or a Noether origin for the conditional conservation law.
